# 🚀 Fin2Edge
###  Financial Literacy Platform

> Empowering users to build smarter financial habits through personalized learning in **Saving, Investing, and Budgeting**.

🌐 **Live Demo:** - https://fin2edge.netlify.app/

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Financial literacy remains a major challenge among students and young professionals. While digital financial services are growing rapidly, many individuals still struggle with:

- Managing monthly budgets
- Building consistent saving habits
- Understanding investments and wealth creation
- Planning for financial emergencies
- Making informed financial decisions

Most existing resources are either too theoretical or lack personalized, hands-on learning experiences.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**Fin2Edge** is an AI-powered financial literacy platform designed to help users master the three pillars of personal finance:

- 💰 Saving
- 📈 Investing
- 📊 Budgeting

At the core of the platform is **Saashya**, an intelligent AI financial mentor that provides personalized guidance based on the user's profession, income, goals, ambitions, and financial behavior.

Rather than simply teaching financial concepts, Fin2Edge enables users to **learn by doing** through interactive simulations, personalized learning paths, and real-world financial scenarios.
