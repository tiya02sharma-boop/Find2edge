## About

**Fin2edge is a single-page, story-driven financial literacy site built for young women exploring personal finance for the first time.

It combines:
- 🏦 **A live 3D bank scene** (Three.js) — an animated banker guides you through the space
- 📚 **Financial Literacy hub** — plain-language breakdowns of savings, loans, insurance, government schemes, and a searchable glossary
- 📈 **A simulated trading exchange** — trade with ₹1,00,000 in play money against a live-moving mock market
- 🎓 **A guided Trading Academy** — a step-by-step walkthrough before you place your first trade
- 🧠 **Recovery quizzes** — lose money in the simulation, and Saashya (your in-app guide) quizzes you back to solvency
- 🎨 **Cinematic, editorial-style design** — gold/navy palette, scroll-triggered animations (GSAP), and a persistent floating guide character
